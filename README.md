# Open Rockets Press

## Overview
Open Rockets Press is a document hosting and publication platform designed to empower youth-led contributions. It serves as a dedicated space where students and minors can publish their own work, ranging from books and research papers to magazines and club posters. 

## Vision
The core mission of Open Rockets Press is to protect and prioritize minors' intellectual property. Often, young creators' works are lost or not taken seriously. By providing a legitimate publishing platform, we aim to:
- Preserve youth-created literature, research, and projects.
- Equip publications with professional identifiers (e.g., DOIs - Digital Object Identifiers) to give them proper recognition.
- Offer protective licensing models (like CC BY, CC0, or a custom Open Rockets non-derivative license) to ensure young contributors retain ownership of their work.

## How it Works
1. **Submission:** Contributors can upload various types of documents (books, journals, articles, posters).
2. **Mandatory Check:** Every submission goes through a mandatory moderation process to ensure it meets the community standards and holds real value.
3. **Publication:** Once approved, the document is published on the platform (e.g., at `press.openrockets.com`) with its unique identifier and chosen license.

## Future Roadmap
- Automate the publishing workflow using modern web technologies (e.g., Cloudflare Workers, Supabase).
- Implement a streamlined identifier system for all published documents.
- Build a supportive ecosystem that encourages students to share their work without the fear of their intellectual property being exploited.
